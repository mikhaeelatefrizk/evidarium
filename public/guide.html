01

Start with a research question

Evidarium keeps the question, sources, original files, decisions, quotations and analysis record connected. Start a project, save a clear question and eligibility criteria, then bring in a small set of evidence you can inspect.

  1. Define the project. Record the question, criteria and whether external searches are allowed.
  2. Collect the evidence. Search or import references, preserve originals, and inspect the extracted text.
  3. Make reviewable decisions. Record screening reasons, exact quotations and the analysis you approve.
  4. Preserve the record. Download the project interchange, original files and independent-review archive separately.
Choose your interface language. The workspace has separate English and Arabic modes. Language changes the interface, not your titles, quotations, numbers or research text. Each remains in its original language.

Hosted workspace

Sign in with ChatGPT for a private project. This establishes identity; it does not enable hosted AI inference or imply that an AI subscription is required for the research workflow. Search, storage, native analysis, review and exports are separate capabilities.

Local workspace

Run the interface on your own computer for local storage and optional specialist engines. Local and hosted projects are separate; transfer is explicit. The standalone local edition has one identity and cannot provide two independent reviewers.

02

From a source to an inspectable claim

Search and preserve

Search Europe PMC, PubMed or Crossref, inspect the metadata, and save the relevant records. Searches return up to 20 records per query and are exploratory; they do not establish a complete systematic search. Record the database, strategy, date and restrictions in your protocol.

Import RIS or CSL JSON through preview and duplicate review. A duplicate retains the existing record and its decisions. Bibliography exports support RIS, BibTeX and CSL JSON. Imported metadata is externally supplied and is not automatically verified evidence.

Read the original

Upload the original, then prepare and approve extraction. Stored does not mean read. Open the extracted text and inspect page coverage and warnings against the original. Resume an interrupted upload by selecting the same original file: its name, size and checksummed parts are checked before new parts are sent. A changed file needs a new upload.

Open-access Europe PMC retrieval preserves the original XML, source identifiers, retrieval details, licence and checksum. The bounded reader extracts abstract/body paragraphs, headings and flattened table rows; it does not interpret figures, equation layout, references or supplementary files. It accepts at most 6 MiB of XML and extracts at most 240,000 characters or 2,000 blocks. External-search restrictions also block this retrieval.

Attach exact evidence

A claim receipt records the statement, a source or extracted file, an exact location, a verbatim quotation and whether it supports, contradicts or provides context. The server checks the quotation against the selected stored text. A researcher must still accept or reject the interpretation. A matching passage, working link or checksum does not prove scientific validity, causality or study quality.

Project search covers indexed abstracts, extracted passages, conversations, notes, drafts, results and claims. It uses BM25 keyword matching, not semantic understanding or a complete reading. Snippets may omit intervening text. Conversation history is displayed in pages of 100 messages. Commands /search terms, /find terms and /note text provide search, project retrieval and notes.

03

Independent review, with a preserved record

Save eligibility criteria in the brief or structured protocol before assigning screening. The protocol also records population, intervention/exposure, comparators, outcomes, designs, databases, search strategy, extraction and disagreement plans, risk-of-bias assessment, synthesis and registration.

  1. Invite two distinct researchers. The hosted owner enters a verified account email and copies a single-use invitation link to the intended recipient. No email is sent automatically. The token is shown once and expires after 72 hours.
  2. Prepare the evidence. For full-text screening: upload the original, extract and inspect its text, confirm its checksum, link it to the correct report, then assign the round. One original per report is currently supported.
  3. Review independently. Each round stores an immutable snapshot of criteria, source and available evidence. Each reviewer submits a decision; exclusion requires a reason. The other current decision remains concealed until both submit.
  4. Resolve transparently. Agreement is recorded. If they disagree, the owner records a resolution and reason while preserving both decisions. This is owner resolution, not a third independent review.
  5. Correct through a new round. Submitted decisions cannot be edited. A new round requires a reason and preserves prior rounds. A researcher cannot unsee earlier evidence or decisions, so a repeated round does not restore methodological blinding.
Separate ledgers. Independent outcomes do not overwrite legacy owner screening or its counts. Stage counts reflect each record’s last state within each stage; stages can overlap. They are not additive PRISMA flow counts and do not infer missing search or study-linkage information.

Structured extraction

Link reports to studies manually; a report can belong to one study. Record the extracted field, value, unit, time point and exact quotation at a source/file location. Owner checks require the current exact text match; changed values or evidence must first be saved as unchecked. These checks are not independent dual extraction. Changes retain a reason and the latest 30 revisions.

Archive and access

Download every independent-review archive page using the same archive version. If records change during export, restart from page one. Pages contain up to ten rounds. Concealed decisions remain omitted, including in cancelled rounds; the archive reports these omissions. Team events appear on page one. Tokens, access grants and original files are excluded. Revoking a reviewer preserves history; incomplete affected rounds need cancellation and replacement with active reviewers.

04

Approve the analysis you can inspect

Choose the actual input and parameters, prepare the task, inspect its input fingerprint in Activity, then approve execution. Results, errors, cancellations and interruptions have distinct states. A stored result records execution and provenance; scientific interpretation still needs review.

Analysis available in the website
ToolWhat it does and where it stops
CSV summariesUTF-8 CSV up to 1 MiB. Per-column numeric and missing/non-numeric counts, mean, sample standard deviation, median and range. Up to 50,000 rows, 100 columns and 250,000 cells.

The BCG example CSV uses yi, vi and study columns, with effects on the log risk-ratio scale. Website check runs fixed public numerical references and a generated PDF without AI allowance. A passed reference check does not validate your study design or data.

Optional local specialist engines
  • NumPy: local CSV descriptive statistics.
  • Extraction: default PDF, DOCX, XLSX and text readers; optional Docling. Default limits are 300 PDF pages, 10,000 rows per sheet and two million characters, with coverage warnings. Cached spreadsheet formula results are read, not recalculated.
  • R/metafor: requires R, metafor and jsonlite; the adapter expects independent, comparable effects and does not silently assume independence.
  • ASReview: prioritizes the unreviewed queue from included/excluded seeds at the same stage. It does not change screening decisions.
  • PySR: equation search from a CSV target/features list, with a held-out check. Default 30 iterations; optional Julia setup is substantial. Returned equations are hypotheses, not established findings.
  • INDRA: builds a graph from supplied statement JSON; it is not a PDF reader.
  • PaperQA2: document questions with a separately configured local Ollama model. It does not use the optional ChatGPT conversation connection.
  • OpenScholar: experimental adapter for the upstream repository and its JSON input, GPU and model environment.
  • Biomni: an offline Docker/model recipe; not every upstream dependency or internet tool is available.
  • Custom Python: the exact code is shown for approval and runs in Docker with bounded resources and no network. Input is /work/input; writable output is /output.

Trusted installed adapters run locally with the user’s privileges after approval; generated custom code uses Docker. Engine setup, downloads and contracts are documented in docs/engines.md in the source archive. Local verification covers selected bounded runs, not broad scientific validation of every engine.

External analysis files can be preserved with externally supplied provenance. Evidarium does not claim to have executed or reproduced them. The app does not rent remote hardware or submit remote jobs.

05

Drafts, references and handover

Write manuscripts with saved-source citations and a basic numbered reference list. Revision conflict detection keeps an older browser from overwriting a newer save; download your unsaved text before reloading. Export HTML, Markdown or source JSON. Numbered links do not verify the claims they accompany; this is not a journal-certified citation style or a complete thesis editor.

Project JSON is an interchange file, not a full backup. It can transfer current project records and the included audit information. Imported history is marked as externally supplied and needs review. It does not include original bytes, complete manuscript/review revision histories, reviewer access or the independent-review ledger. Download originals separately and use the independent-review archive for that ledger. Check third-party content rights before sharing.

For a local backup, close Evidarium and preserve the entire ~/.evidarium data directory. Operating-system credentials are not in that directory and are not included in a file backup. Hosted backup and restore still require separate operational verification; a JSON export must not be treated as a recovery guarantee.

06

Set up the local edition

  1. Install Node.js 24.19 or newer and Python 3.11 or newer.
  2. Download and extract the local application ZIP.
  3. On macOS/Linux run bash start.sh. On Windows run ./start-windows.ps1 in PowerShell, following your organization’s execution policy.
  4. Keep the terminal open and visit http://127.0.0.1:4317. Do not expose this local port as a multi-user service.

The launcher creates a project virtual environment with pinned base Python packages. Specialist engines and model weights are separate, explicit installations. Examples: python scripts/install-engine.py asreview, with indra, pysr or docling as alternatives. PySR/Docling and their dependencies can require several GiB; R is installed separately.

Custom Python needs the documented Docker image evidarium-analysis:0.1.0. Example task bounds are 120 seconds, 2 GiB memory and 2 CPUs; inspect the proposed code and parameters before approval. On restart, unfinished runs are marked interrupted instead of being reported as complete. Interrupted uploads can be explicitly resumed or removed.

07

Optional model connection and its boundaries

The existing local connector is optional. Product-wide AI provision and pricing are not settled in this release; they are not a prerequisite for organizing evidence, reviewing or running native website calculations. Hosted subscription inference remains disabled.

The local connector uses the official open-source OpenAI authorization flow with PKCE. It checks identity, plan permission and token responses, stores credentials in the operating-system credential store and offers the actual account model catalogue. Tokens do not enter the web workspace. Choose an available model explicitly; the app does not silently substitute models or fall back to paid API billing.

A streamed response is complete only after the provider confirms completion. Cancellation, errors and budget exhaustion retain partial text with an incomplete state. Eligibility, available models and context remain account/provider dependent. The installation has one registered account per data directory, without an account chooser. Website identity, local authorization and hosted inference are separate.

The optional connected-workflow check requires explicit consent, creates a separate synthetic QA project, executes actual calculations and requests two cited model turns using the connected plan allowance. It is not represented as already validated on your account. Citation reports check record existence, exact locations, metadata-only access and whether evidence was supplied to that run; they do not check that a claim follows from the evidence.

See the official token-sharing documentation for the authorization protocol. Advanced engines can have separate local model requirements.

08

Know where your research goes

Hosted project records use the application database and original files use object storage. Local projects use local disk. Optional model requests send selected context to OpenAI; literature searches send queries to the selected provider. An external engine may have its own explicitly configured dependencies and data path.

Disabling external searches in the brief blocks manual and assistant literature searches and full-text retrieval. It does not disable the model connection, recall prior disclosures or make previous requests private retroactively. Review the material included before using an external service.

The source does not include an analytics SDK, private user records, credentials or full research texts. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption, zero retention, institutional compliance or an independent security audit. Deployment access, backups and provider terms need their own assessment.

There is no universal document parser, unlimited context, automatic cloud synchronization, universal malware scanner, remote hardware rental, journal submission or autonomous research degree. Collaboration currently covers the bounded independent-screening workflow; it is not a complete institutional research management system.

10

Verification and open source

The source archive includes the application, schema, local authorization flow, analysis adapters, Docker recipe, tests, documentation and security notices. Original project code is MIT licensed; dependencies retain their own licences and terms.

Engineering checks cover account isolation, cross-origin protection, checksummed uploads and recovery, deduplication, screening history, import/export, explicit task approval, failure handling, evidence receipts, numeric bounds, streaming completion and worker execution. These are bounded tests, not a guarantee of perfect correctness or scientific validity.

Readiness is not assumed. The independent-review browser check uses synthetic identities. A hosted test with two real accounts and hosted backup/restore verification remain required. Real account model authorization/inference and optional heavy engines need environment-specific validation; PaperQA2, OpenScholar and Biomni require further acceptance work.
